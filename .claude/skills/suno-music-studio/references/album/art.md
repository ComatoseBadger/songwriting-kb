# Album Art Direction

How to turn an album concept into a cover: visual direction, style and color choices by album type and genre, image-generator prompts for each platform, typography, and delivery specs.

Discuss the visual concept during album planning (Phase 5 in references/album/concept.md). Generate the final artwork once the tracks are final, so the art reflects the finished record. If the user has stated visual preferences (styles, palette, composition, platform), they win.

---

## Principles

- **The cover is visual storytelling.** It's the first thing people see. It should communicate the album's essence instantly, work at both thumbnail and full size, be memorable and distinctive, and complement the music rather than compete with it.
- **Less is more.** One clear focal point, no clutter, negative space, reads in a glance.
- **Prompts need precision.** Be specific without over-constraining. Use visual language, not musical concepts ("rusted tank over tenements", not "a sad folk song"). Guide composition and mood, then iterate.
- **Genre informs but doesn't dictate.** Honor genre expectations or subvert them, but do it on purpose.

---

## Workflow

1. **Develop the concept.** Answer: What's the album about? Who's the audience, and what does the genre lead them to expect? What should the first impression feel like? Which images come from the lyrics or concept? Warm or cool, saturated or muted? Write the result as a 2-3 sentence concept.
2. **Propose 2-3 directions** with pros and cons for each. Let the user pick or redirect.
3. **Pick the platform.** Ask which image generator the user uses (Midjourney, Leonardo.ai, DALL-E/ChatGPT, Stable Diffusion, or another) unless they've already said. The prompt format differs a lot between them.
4. **Gather references:** covers in the genre, art movements, photography styles, palettes.
5. **Plan the composition:**
   - Layout: centered, rule of thirds, symmetric or asymmetric
   - Focal point: what the eye hits first
   - Depth: shallow (isolated subject), deep (environmental), or flat (graphic)
   - Aspect: always square 1:1
6. **Build the prompt** in the platform's format (below).
7. **Iterate.** Start with 4 variations using slightly different prompts. Expect 3-5 rounds before a final. For each round, check that it works as a thumbnail, communicates the concept immediately, is distinctive, and fits the genre without cliché.
8. **Deliver** the final prompt (with a negative prompt where the platform supports one), the platform settings, and notes on what to change if the results miss.

## Approaches by Album Type

**Documentary / true crime**: authenticity and gravity.
- *Archival:* vintage-photo look, newspaper clippings, evidence photos, redacted documents, court files, black and white or desaturated.
- *Symbolic:* objects from the story (handcuffs, scales, a computer), locations (courtroom, headquarters), an abstract version of the key moment.
- *Portrait-based:* a stylized portrait, silhouette, or partial face of the key figure. Avoid literal photos because of rights issues.
- Example: *Concept:* industrial negligence looming over an immigrant neighborhood. *Visual:* a massive rusted tank towering over cramped tenements, dark sepia and brown, 1919 Boston, the ominous calm before disaster. *Style:* vintage photograph meets punk rock, gritty, high contrast.

**Thematic / concept (non-narrative)**: mood and metaphor.
- *Abstract/atmospheric:* color fields and textures, emotion without a literal subject.
- *Symbolic/metaphorical:* one central symbol, surreal combinations, visual poetry.
- *Typographic:* the title itself as the image, with custom lettering.
- Example: *Concept:* letting go. *Visual:* open hands releasing glowing particles into a sunset sky, particles dissolving into light, close-up, bokeh background. *Style:* cinematic photography, warm tones, gentle movement.

**Character study**: identity and presence.
- *Stylized portrait:* an illustrated or interpretive likeness, not photoreal.
- *Symbolic objects:* the person's items, workspace, hands, tools, signature elements.
- *Silhouette/shadow:* a recognizable form without detail, which adds mystery and avoids rights issues.

**Narrative / storytelling**: the key moment or the journey.
- *Climactic scene:* one powerful moment with dramatic lighting.
- *Journey metaphor:* a path or road, contrasting beginning and end.
- *Character in context:* the protagonist in their world, telling the story through the environment.

**Collection**: a unifying element.
- *Thematic unity:* one visual motif and a consistent palette across standalone songs.
- *Grid/collage:* several images showing the variety, as organized chaos.
- *Abstract pattern:* non-representational but cohesive, mood over meaning.

**OST**: immersion in the world, with an aesthetic that fits the medium.
- *Media UI aesthetic:* pixel frames and HUD elements (games), film-strip borders or a clapperboard (film), episode cards (TV, anime). CRT scanlines and neon-on-dark for retro games, grain and vignette for noir, cel-shading for anime. Typography from the fictional medium's era.
- *World landscape:* sweeping environment art (a vista, a dungeon entrance, a neon city, a noir alley) like concept art or a film poster.
- *Iconic item:* one meaningful object (sword, badge, controller, mask) as a clean focal point, with glow or particle trails.
- Example (sci-fi platformer): a pixel-art spaceship flying through a neon star field, trailing musical notes that dissolve into pixels, CRT scanlines, deep-space purple and electric cyan, centered, a retro health bar at the top. Pixel art meets synthwave.
- Example (noir film): a rain-slicked street at night, a lone streetlamp, a saxophone silhouette in a foggy window above, high-contrast black and white with amber highlights, vintage movie-poster composition with art deco type. Film-noir photography with cinematic grain.

## Visual Style Reference

### Photography styles

| Style | Effect | Use for |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Editorial | Professional, magazine quality | Documentary, true crime |
| Portrait | Subject-focused, intimate | Character studies |
| Street | Candid, urban, raw | Hip-hop, gritty rock |
| Fashion | Stylized, dramatic light | Pop, R&B, high concept |
| Documentary | Authentic, journalistic | True stories, history |
| Fine art | Intentional, artistic | Concept albums, experimental |

### Illustration styles

| Style | Effect | Use for |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Digital illustration | Clean, modern | Tech, contemporary |
| Vintage poster | Retro, nostalgic | Historical, throwback |
| Comic book | Bold, dynamic | Narrative, action |
| Minimalist | Simple, iconic | Abstract concepts |
| Surrealist painting | Dreamlike, symbolic | Experimental, avant-garde |
| Technical illustration | Precise, detailed | Tech, engineering |

### Art movements

| Movement | Aesthetic | Use for |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Film noir | High contrast, shadow, mystery | Crime, thriller |
| Cyberpunk | Neon, futuristic, gritty | Tech, dystopia |
| Art deco | Geometric, luxurious, golden age | Sophisticated, classic |
| Brutalism | Raw concrete, stark | Industrial, harsh |
| Vaporwave | Retro-futurist pastels, surreal | Electronic, ironic |
| Minimalism | Essential, clean | Abstract, meditative |

### Color psychology

- **Dark/serious** (true crime, heavy topics): black, charcoal, deep navy. Desaturated, with few bright accents and high contrast. A single accent color (such as red on black and white) draws focus.
- **Energetic/aggressive** (rock, hip-hop): red, orange, electric blue. High saturation, sharp contrasts.
- **Calm/introspective** (folk, ambient): earth tones (brown, tan, moss) or pastels (soft pink, blue, cream). Low saturation, gentle gradients.
- **Futuristic/electronic** (EDM, synth): neon cyan, magenta, electric purple on deep black, with glow and a synthetic feel.
- **Nostalgic/vintage** (country, classic): faded sepia and washed denim, warm yellows and oranges, muted reds and greens, film grain.

### Genre visual language

- **Hip-hop:** bold type, street photography, gold, black, and red, urban settings and luxury items, dramatic light.
- **Electronic/EDM:** geometric shapes, fractals, neon glow, abstract and futuristic, motion blur.
- **Rock/alternative:** raw, gritty textures, live or candid band photos, analog grain, muted or saturated color.
- **Folk/acoustic:** nature and landscapes, warm earth tones, a handmade, organic feel, intimate close-ups.
- **Country:** Americana (roads, fields, skylines), vintage trucks, boots, guitars, sunset and dusty tones.

For more genre-specific notes, see references/genres/<genre-slug>/README.md.

## Prompt Construction

**Anatomy (all platforms):** subject → style → mood/lighting → color palette → composition → technical quality. Always say it's square album cover art.

### Platform formats

| Platform | Prompt style | Negatives | Key difference |
|----------|--------------|-----------|----------------|
| **Midjourney** | Comma-separated tags + parameters | `--no` flag | Keyword-driven, concise, strong artistic interpretation |
| **Leonardo.ai** | Natural-language sentences | Separate field | Descriptive, predictable, good for photoreal and cinematic |
| **DALL-E / ChatGPT** | Conversational instruction | Not supported | State what you want, never what to avoid. Best text rendering. |
| **Stable Diffusion** | Weighted tags `(keyword:1.2)` | Separate, extensive | Maximum control: LoRAs, CFG, samplers, local generation |

**Midjourney**
```
[Subject], [style], [mood/lighting], [color palette], [composition],
[technical details], album cover art --ar 1:1 --v 6
```
- `--ar 1:1` makes it square. Use the current model version flag. `--style raw` gives more literal output. `--s 50` reduces stylization and `--s 250` increases it.
- `--no text watermark` for exclusions. Keep the prompt under about 60 words, because Midjourney ignores the excess.

**Leonardo.ai**
```
Prompt: [Full-sentence description of scene, style, mood, colors, composition,
         as if describing the image to another person.]
Negative Prompt: [exclusions, comma-separated]
Model: Phoenix (versatile) / Kino XL (cinematic) / SDXL (control)
Preset: Cinematic / Dynamic / Photography
Aspect Ratio: 1:1
```
- Write sentences, not tags, and put exclusions in the negative field, not the main prompt.
- Guidance scale 7-9 is balanced, and higher sticks closer to the prompt. Alchemy mode raises quality at a credit cost.
- Include "album cover artwork" or "square format artwork" in the prompt.

**DALL-E**
```
Create a square album cover artwork showing [detailed scene].
The style should be [approach] with [mood/lighting].
Use [palette] colors. Frame the composition [composition details].
```
- Phrase it as a clear instruction and name the style explicitly. Because there are no negatives, describe only what should appear.

**Stable Diffusion**
```
Prompt: [subject], [style], [mood], [colors], [composition],
        (album cover art:1.2), (high quality:1.1), 4k
Negative: (worst quality:1.4), (low quality:1.4), blurry, watermark, text,
          deformed, [genre-inappropriate elements]
Steps: 30-50 | CFG: 7-9 | Sampler: DPM++ 2M Karras (or Euler a)
```
- Weight the key elements, for example `(rusted tank:1.3)`. An extensive negative prompt improves quality a lot.

**Adapting between platforms:** for DALL-E, make the Leonardo version more conversational. For Stable Diffusion, start from the Midjourney tags and add weights and a fuller negative prompt.

### Refinement keywords

| Goal | Add |
|------|-----|
| Simplify | minimalist, clean composition, negative space, single focal point |
| Drama | dramatic lighting, high contrast, cinematic, bold |
| Realism | photorealistic, high detail, 8k, octane render |
| Stylize | stylized, artistic interpretation, painterly, a named art movement |
| Color | exact hex codes, "muted palette", "vibrant", "monochrome with [color] accent" |

## Prompt Examples

**Tech documentary (Linux distros)**: *concept:* distributions as an evolving ecosystem.
```
Midjourney:
Abstract digital tree made of circuit board traces, roots formed from command line code,
branches forming a penguin silhouette, gradient from green terminal glow to orange to
deep red, dark background fading to black, isometric view, technical illustration style,
clean and modern, high detail, 4k, album cover art --ar 1:1 --v 6

Leonardo:
Prompt: An abstract digital tree whose trunk and branches are made of glowing circuit
board traces. The roots dissolve into lines of command-line code. The canopy forms the
silhouette of a penguin. The palette shifts from green terminal glow at the base through
warm orange to deep red at the crown, against pure black. Slightly elevated isometric
angle. Clean, modern technical illustration with high detail.
Negative Prompt: photorealistic, blurry, text, watermark, human figures, messy wires,
low quality, pixelated
Model: Phoenix | Preset: Dynamic | 1:1
```
It works because it names a specific subject, a clear style, defined colors, and the composition.

**True crime (corporate scandal)**: *concept:* hidden corporate corruption.
```
Midjourney:
Corporate boardroom from dramatic low angle, empty leather chairs around polished table,
single overhead light creating harsh shadows, document with redacted text in foreground,
film noir style, high contrast black and white with single red accent, cinematic lighting,
photorealistic, moody and ominous, 4k, album cover art --ar 1:1 --v 6

Leonardo:
Prompt: A corporate boardroom photographed from a dramatic low angle. Empty black leather
chairs surround a polished mahogany table. A single harsh overhead light casts deep
shadows. In the foreground, a document with redacted black bars lies on the table. High-
contrast black and white with a single red accent, perhaps the redaction ink or a pen.
Film noir atmosphere, moody and ominous, photorealistic cinematic quality.
Negative Prompt: people, faces, colorful, bright, cheerful, cartoon, illustration,
blurry, low resolution, watermark
Model: Kino XL | Preset: Cinematic | 1:1
```

**Electronic/ambient**: *concept:* digital consciousness.
```
Midjourney:
Flowing data streams forming abstract humanoid silhouette, particles of light coalescing
and dispersing, deep blue and cyan palette with electric purple accents, dark void
background, motion blur, digital art, ethereal and mysterious, centered composition,
```

**Folk/acoustic**: *concept:* journey, solitude, natural beauty.
```

```

## Text & Typography

**Include text when** the title is essential to the concept, the typography *is* the visual, or the genre expects it (punk and metal often are text-heavy).

**Skip text when** the image speaks for itself, the text will be added digitally later, or the cover is stronger without it. Generators often garble lettering, so the safer default is to generate the image without text and set the type afterward. DALL-E renders text best if text has to be in the generation.

**Best practices**
- High contrast against the background
- Legible at thumbnail size, in a clear typeface
- Placed in the top third or bottom third
- Album title and artist name only. No tracklist, year, or extras.
- Match the type to the world: art deco for noir, pixel fonts for retro games, hand lettering for folk.

## Multi-Album Series

Keep consistent: the palette family, composition style, a recognizable recurring motif, and the type family.
Vary: the subject matter, specific colors within the palette, and a new focal point each time.

## Specs & the Thumbnail Test

| Destination | Size | Notes |
|-------------|------|-------|
| Streaming distributors (Spotify, Apple Music, etc.) | **3000 x 3000 px minimum**, 4000 x 4000 recommended | Square 1:1, RGB, PNG or JPEG, 72 DPI minimum (300 recommended), no blur or pixelation |
| SoundCloud | 800 x 800 px minimum | High contrast helps in feeds |

Generators often output at lower resolution than 3000 px. Upscale the final image before delivery and check the upscaled result for artifacts.

**Thumbnail test:** the art must read at 200 x 200 px or smaller, and Spotify shows it at about 300 x 300. Shrink it and check: Can you still tell what it is? Does it stand out in a grid? Is the text readable? If not, simplify, cut detail, and increase contrast.

## Rights & Sensitive Content

**Avoid:** real people's faces (unless public domain or licensed), copyrighted logos and brands, trademarked characters and symbols, and prompts naming living artists' work as the style to copy.

**Use instead:** stylized interpretations, silhouettes instead of faces, generic objects instead of branded ones, original symbols.

**Dark and true-crime topics:** don't glorify violence. Symbolic beats literal. Keep it tasteful, and consider the victims' families. For historical events, research visual sensitivities and avoid stereotypes.

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Too busy | One strong element plus negative space |
| Cliché (a mic for hip-hop, a guitar for rock) | Find an unusual angle on the familiar symbol, or use a metaphor |
| Doesn't scale | Test at thumbnail size early and simplify |
| Wrong mood (cheerful art on a serious album) | Align color, lighting, and style with the music's mood |
| Too literal (an album about cars shows a car) | Symbolic representation, an unusual angle, an interpretive style |
| Text overload | Title and artist at most, or none |

---

## Final Checklist

- [ ] Reads at 200 x 200 px
- [ ] Communicates the album's mood immediately
- [ ] Distinctive and memorable, fits the genre without cliché
- [ ] 3000 x 3000 px minimum, square, RGB
- [ ] No rights problems (faces, logos, trademarks)
- [ ] Text, if any, renders cleanly and is legible
- [ ] The user approves
